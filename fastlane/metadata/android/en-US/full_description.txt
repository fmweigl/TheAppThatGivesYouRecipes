Not sure what to cook? YetAnotherMealsApp shows you a random recipe from TheMealDB's community collection with every tap.

Each recipe comes with:
• A photo of the dish
• Its category and cuisine
• The ingredients with their measures
• Step-by-step instructions

Tap Next for another idea, or Previous to go back to a recipe you've already seen.

Made with care:
• Light and dark theme that follows your system settings
• Works with screen readers like TalkBack
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app collects no personal data at all.

Open source:

Recipe data and imagery: TheMealDB (themealdb.com). TheMealDB is an open, crowd-sourced recipe database. This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by TheMealDB.

Recipes are contributed by the community. Always check ingredients yourself, especially for allergies or dietary requirements.
