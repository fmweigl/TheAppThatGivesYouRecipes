Not sure what to cook? The App That Gives You Recipes shows you a random recipe with every tap, finds recipes by name when you know what you want, and keeps the ones you like.

Each recipe comes with:
• A photo of the dish
• Its category and cuisine
• The ingredients with their measures
• Step-by-step instructions

Tap Next for another idea, or Previous to go back to a recipe you've already seen.

Looking for something specific? Search recipes by name in their own tab, for example "tomato" or "curry", and open any result. Results appear as you type. What you type is sent to TheMealDB to find matching recipes; the app keeps no search history.

Found one you like? Tap the heart to save it. Your favorites have their own tab and open even without an internet connection. Removed one by mistake? Tap Undo.

Want to cook it with someone? Share a recipe with its ingredients and instructions as text, to a messenger, an email or your notes.

Made with care:
• Light and dark theme that follows your system settings
• Works with screen readers like TalkBack
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app collects no personal data at all.
• Your favorites are stored only on your device.
